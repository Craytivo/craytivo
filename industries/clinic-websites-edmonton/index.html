Clinic website design Edmonton FAQ

How much does clinic website design cost in Edmonton?

Most clinic website projects start at $450 and scale based on page count, booking setup, and service-page depth.

Can booking flows be split for new patients, returning patients, and consultation requests?

Yes. Separate booking paths usually improve conversion because each patient type sees a clearer, lower-friction next step.

How should intake forms handle privacy and consent expectations?

Collect only essential details first, include clear consent language, and route sensitive intake through your approved clinic workflow.

Can a clinic website show practitioner specialties and availability without feeling crowded?

Yes. Structured practitioner cards and service-specific bios keep credentials, specialties, and booking options easy to scan.

Should treatment pages target conditions, services, or both?

Usually both. This captures service intent plus condition searches from Downtown Edmonton, South Edmonton, and nearby communities.

Can we include insurance or direct-billing details to pre-qualify inquiries?

Yes. Place insurance and direct-billing details near booking CTAs so Edmonton patients understand coverage expectations before inquiry.

How quickly can a clinic website launch and then be refined using booking data?

Fast-track builds can launch in as little as 4 days. Most full clinic websites run 2 to 4 weeks, then refine around local demand periods like January intake and year-end benefits usage.

Do we own the site and get support after launch?

Yes. You keep ownership of your website assets, and support is available for updates and growth.
